# Instagram step by step (English UI)

Your app is in English; your audience is in Spanish. Keep the **captions in Spanish** and follow the **English labels** below.

---

## 0. Before you start

- Instagram app updated.
- Facebook Page already created.
- 3 images saved on your phone: `tarjeta-marca.png`, `tarjeta-zona.png`, `tarjeta-checklist.png`.
- 20 minutes.

---

## 1. Switch to a professional account and connect the Page

1. Open your **Profile**.
2. Tap the menu **☰** (top right).
3. Tap **Settings and privacy**.
4. Scroll to **Account type and tools**.
5. Tap **Switch to professional account**.
6. Choose **Business** → **Continue**.
7. Category: **Education**.
8. Tap **Connect Facebook Page** and select *El mercado invisible · La Habana*.

If you already have a professional account, skip to step 2.

---

## 2. Complete your profile

Tap **Edit profile**.

| Field (English) | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile photo** | `og.png` |
| **Name** | Mercado 2 ruedas · La Habana |
| **Username** | same as Telegram, e.g. `mercadodosruedas` |
| **Bio** | “El mercado de motos, bicis y patinetes de La Habana en datos · 4.979 anuncios · Gratis ↓” |
| **Links** | your site URL |
| **Category** | Education |

The **Name** field is searchable: include “motos” and “La Habana”.

---

## 3. First carousel (today, 11:30)

1. Tap **+** at the bottom.
2. Choose **Post**.
3. Select the 3 images in this order:
   1. `tarjeta-marca.png` (45,5%).
   2. `tarjeta-zona.png` (2,7×).
   3. `tarjeta-checklist.png` (10 cambios).
4. Tap **Next** (filters) → **Next** again.
5. Paste the caption in Spanish:

> El 45,5% de las motos en venta en La Habana no dice su marca.
> El 43,3% no tiene ni una cifra en el título.
> Y hay 2,7 veces de diferencia de precio entre municipios.
>
> No es un problema de precio: es de información. Y se arregla gratis.
>
> Analicé 4.979 anuncios. Resumen, calculadora y checklist gratis en el enlace de la bio.
>
> #motoslahabana #motoselectricas #cuba #revolico #deliverycuba #bicicletas #lahabana

6. Tap **Share**.

**Important:** links in captions are not clickable. The link goes in your **bio**.

---

## 4. Stories and highlights

1. Open the post → **Share to story**.
2. Add text: “45,5% no dice su marca”.
3. Add a **Link** sticker if available.
4. Second story: before/after (“MOTO SE VENDE” vs “AVA 100 km, Centro Habana”).
5. Third story: “Checklist gratis en el enlace”.
6. Open a story → **Highlight** → name it:
   - **Precios**
   - **Guía**
   - **Estudio**

Stories cannot be scheduled; post them manually.

---

## 5. Schedule posts (Meta Business Suite, English UI)

1. Go to `business.facebook.com`.
2. Open **Planner**.
3. Tap **Create post**.
4. Select **Instagram**.
5. Upload the image or reel, paste the Spanish caption.
6. Tap **Schedule** → choose date and time.
7. Confirm.

You can also use the **Meta Business Suite** app.

---

## 6. Rhythm

- **3 posts per week**: 1 carousel, 1 data post, 1 reel.
- **3 stories per week**.
- Reply to comments and DMs within 24 hours.
- Follow 20 local accounts: repair shops, delivery riders, cycling, stores.
- Comment with data on other posts, no links.

---

## 7. Common issues (English UI)

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| No **Switch to professional account** | Update the app; check **Settings and privacy → Account type and tools** |
| No clickable link in caption | Use **Links** in your bio |
| Carousel fails | All images must have the same aspect ratio (1080×1350) |
| Low reach | Post reels and stories; avoid links in captions |
| Few followers | Do not buy followers; use reels and collaborate with 3 local accounts |
| Hashtags | 5–10 relevant ones, not 30 |
